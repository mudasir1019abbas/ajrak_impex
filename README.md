# Ajrak Impex International

Ajrak Impex International is a full-stack company website for presenting the business, its
products and services, and its global trade activities.

The site includes company and team information, news, contact inquiries, and authenticated
customer accounts. Administrators can review customer accounts and manage incoming inquiries
through a dedicated dashboard.

## Technology

- Next.js 16 App Router and React 19
- JavaScript
- Tailwind CSS 4
- MySQL for customer accounts and contact inquiries
